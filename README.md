## The country

The **Republic of Aetheria** is a cloud nation of floating sky-islands that
exists only above 3,000 metres. Its citizens drift between the islands by
airship, and its passports are stamped by the winds themselves.

## The passport

A storybook-style illustrated spread for Aetheria's most seasoned traveller,
aviator **Elara Mireille Vane** (born 14 MAR 1996, Cirrus City; passport
AET-000314, issued 02 FEB 2024, expires 02 FEB 2034):

- **Data page** — a hand-drawn portrait, her details, the national emblem of
  three sky-islands, and a machine-readable zone.
- **Visa page** — entry stamps from Terra Firma, Stratos, and Cumulus
  (2024–2026), a cloud-whale drifting guardian, and one large red stamp that
  tells the whole story: **VALID ONLY ABOVE 3,000 M — HOLDER PERMITTED TO
  DRIFT**.
